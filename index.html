Sec. 01 — What we do

Three things we build every semester.

From technical workshops to social nights, chapter programming is planned around what actually helps members grow — on paper and on a team.

01 / Skills

Hands-on workshops

CAD, manufacturing, and design-review sessions led by upperclassmen and industry engineers — the skills that don't fit in a syllabus.

02 / Network

Industry nights

Site tours, recruiter panels, and info sessions with the companies hiring UIC engineers — Boeing, Caterpillar, IUOE, and more.

03 / Community

Socials & projects

Design-build teams, study nights, and socials that turn a lecture hall of strangers into a chapter that shows up for each other.
