Our Project Teams

5 teams, 5 ways to build something real.

Early-stage prototypes and experimentation.

R&D Team is an innovation-focused group dedicated to exploring new engineering concepts, testing emerging technologies, and developing early-stage prototypes. Members collaborate on experimental projects, validate ideas through research and hands-on testing, and turn creative concepts into practical engineering solutions that can grow into future ASME initiatives.

Human-powered, engineered for endurance.

The Electric/Human-Powered Vehicle Challenge team designs, builds, and races a fully custom vehicle each year — covering frame fabrication, drivetrain design, and aerodynamics from CAD to the finish line.

From chassis to checkered flag.

The Go-Kart team builds a competition-ready kart from the ground up, taking on suspension geometry, engine tuning, and race-day strategy alongside a full season of testing.

Engineering solutions that outlast the semester.

The Sustainability team develops projects focused on renewable energy, resource efficiency, and campus impact — turning environmental research into deployable engineering.

Design, build, destroy.

The BattleBots team designs and fabricates combat robots for regional competitions, iterating fast on armor, drive systems, and weaponry between every match.
