Workshop · Sep 18

Engineering Responsibilities Workshop

A joint session with IUOE Local 150 covering professional and ethical responsibility in mechanical design — where a signature carries liability, how field engineers document decisions, and what "responsible charge" actually means once you're the one signing off on a drawing. Open to all majors; no RSVP required for members.
Thursday, September 18 · 6:00 PM – 7:30 PM
Doors open at 5:45 PM. Light dinner provided for the first 60 attendees.
Science & Engineering Offices (SEO), Room 218 — 851 S Morgan St, Chicago. Enter through the east lobby; signage will direct you from the elevators.
RSVP'd so far: 74 members across mechanical, industrial, and civil engineering. Capacity is 120 — walk-ins welcome until the room fills.
