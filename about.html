Our mission

Engineers who show up for each other.

UIC ASME exists to connect mechanical engineering students with the skills, people, and opportunities that a classroom alone can't provide — and to make a large campus feel a little smaller.

Sec. 01 — History

A chapter built by students, for students.

Chartered through UIC's Department of Mechanical & Industrial Engineering as the local section of the national ASME society.

Founded

A student-run charter

Run entirely by an elected e-board, with faculty advisors supporting from the department each semester.

Affiliation

Part of national ASME

Members get access to ASME's national resources, conferences, and career network alongside chapter events.

Open to

Every major, every year

Most active members study mechanical or industrial engineering, but the chapter is open campus-wide.

Sec. 02 — Membership

Join the chapter

Fill out the form and we'll add you to the chapter mailing list — dues and committee sign-ups happen at your first general body meeting.

  • 01 Open to all UIC students, any major, any year.
  • 02 First meeting is free — no commitment required.
  • 03 Semester dues cover socials, workshop materials, and swag.

You're on the list — check your UIC email for next steps.
